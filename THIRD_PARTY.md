# Third-party methods

The AI skills adapt the *methods and prompt ideas* (no code) of these open-source projects:

- **TradingAgents** by TauricResearch (Apache-2.0): multi-agent analyst → debate → risk-manager workflow,
  used in `.claude/skills/analyst-team/`. https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents
- **ai-hedge-fund** by virattt (MIT): investor-persona agents combined by a portfolio manager,
  used in `.claude/skills/investor-panel/`. https://github.com/virattt/ai-hedge-fund
- **daily_stock_analysis** by ZhuLinsen (MIT): per-holding verdict digest format,
  used in `.claude/skills/daily-digest/`. https://github.com/ZhuLinsen/daily_stock_analysis
